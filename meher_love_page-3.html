Meher, Agar meri zindagi ek kahani hai, toh uske sabse khoobsurat panne tumse jude hain. ❤️ Tumse milna, tumse baatein karna, tumhari chhoti-chhoti baaton par smile karna… pata nahi kab tum meri aadat se meri zarurat ban gayi. Jo waqt humne saath guzara, woh mere liye sirf memories nahi hain. Woh meri zindagi ka woh hissa hain jise main hamesha dil ke kareeb rakhunga. Haan, mujhse galtiyan hui hain. Maine tumhe hurt kiya… aur kaash main woh waqt wapas laakar sab kuch sahi kar pata. Main tumse koi promise nahi karwa raha, na tum par koi pressure hai. Bas dil ki ek baat keh raha hoon — aaj bhi jab main “sukoon” sochta hoon, toh kahin na kahin tum yaad aati ho. ❤️ Aur agar kabhi tumhe bhi lage ki humari kahani abhi poori nahi hui… toh main wahi milunga, jahan tumne mujhe chhoda tha. ❤️‍🩹
